# 2. Build Outbound that Gets Replies

[Back to Capability Map](concept-map)

**The situation:** You have a shortlist of companies you could genuinely help, but no warm path into most of them. Your previous client base isn't the right fit class, so waiting for referrals means waiting indefinitely. You draft a cold email, it reads like a résumé, you send three, hear nothing, and conclude "outbound doesn't work."

**What changes:** You learn outbound as a system, not a personality test: build a target list from a written ICP, write messages that lead with a specific observed problem rather than your credentials, volume and follow-up with discipline, and track reply rate as the metric that matters. You also learn the consultant-specific variants: the referral ask, the "I saw this at your company" trigger-based message, and the content-led warm-up.

**You're ready when:** You have a named list of 50+ target accounts matching a written ICP, a 3-touch sequence you've actually sent, and a reply-rate baseline. You can write a first-touch message in under 15 minutes because the positioning work (Capability 1) already did the heavy lifting.

### Practice This

Write your ICP on one page (industry, size, trigger events, who buys, who blocks). Build a 50-account list from it. Write a 3-touch sequence where touch 1 references a specific observable problem, touch 2 adds one piece of proof, touch 3 is a graceful close. Send to 10 accounts this week. Record reply rate.
