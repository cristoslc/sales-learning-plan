# 9. Manage Pipeline and Forecast Honestly

[Back to Capability Map](concept-map)

**The situation:** You're a solo operator: pipeline is a list of names in a notes app, some of them months old. Some weeks you're too busy to sell and the future quietly empties; other weeks you have nothing *but* selling to do because work just ended. You can't answer the only question that matters for a consultancy: "given current pipeline, what will revenue be in 8 weeks?"

**What changes:** You learn to run pipeline as a small, honest system: defined stages with exit criteria (a stage changes when the *buyer* does something, not when you do), a weekly pipeline review against MEDDPICC evidence, and a personal forecast you'd defend — commit only deals with buyer evidence, and move deals back when evidence weakens [3]. You learn the solo-consultant rhythms: a fixed weekly slot for pipeline work regardless of how busy delivery is, and activity floors (touches per week) so a busy delivery quarter doesn't silently empty next quarter's revenue.

**You're ready when:** You have a stage definition per deal with a last-verified date, and your "commit" deals are ones where the buyer has done something concrete in the last 14 days. You can state your win rate and average cycle length from real data.

### Practice This

Define 4 stages for your consultancy with buyer-action exit criteria (e.g., Stage 2 → 3 only when: quantified pain stated by buyer AND economic buyer identified). Move your current opportunities to honest stages with last-verified dates. Put a recurring 45-minute pipeline review on your calendar — same time every week.
