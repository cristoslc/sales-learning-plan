# 10. Turn Deals into Referrals and Second Deals

[Back to Capability Map](concept-map)

**The situation:** A project ends well. The client is happy. You say "let me know if you hear of anyone!" and move on. Six months later you're cold again, starting outbound from zero — even though the single richest source of new work (a delighted client with a network full of people like them) was sitting right there, unasked.

**What changes:** You learn to engineer the end of the engagement: a closing review that quantifies delivered value (feeding your positioning and future proposals), a structured referral ask at the moment of maximum goodwill, a reference/case-study agreement while enthusiasm is fresh, and a scheduled re-engagement cadence. You also learn the second-deal lens: the expansion conversation ("now that X works, the adjacent problem Y is much cheaper to fix") is often easier to win than any new client.

**You're ready when:** Every completed engagement ends with four artifacts: a results summary with numbers, a referral ask (made, not implied), a usable testimonial or case study, and a calendar reminder for a check-in in 90 days. Repeat and referral business is a visible line in your pipeline (Capability 9), not a pleasant accident.

### Practice This

Pick your best engagement from the last 2 years. Write the results summary you never wrote (with numbers). Send it to the client with a specific referral ask ("who are two people you know with the same problem?") and offer to help make an introduction easy for them. Schedule the 90-day follow-up.
