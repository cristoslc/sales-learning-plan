# 11. Use AI as a Sales Leverage Tool

[Back to Capability Map](concept-map)

**The situation:** You're one person. Top sellers have managers, enablement teams, call-coaching tools, and researchers. You have a laptop and a calendar. Meanwhile your prospects are increasingly using AI to research vendors before they ever talk to you — which changes what your first conversation has to accomplish [4].

**What changes:** You learn to use AI for the things sales teams pay other humans to do: pre-call research (summarize a target company's public footprint and generate hypothesis pain), role-play (AI plays a skeptical owner-operator while you practice discovery questions), deal review (paste call notes, get a MEDDPICC scorecard draft with reds highlighted), and first-draft outreach (positioning-aware, then humanized). You also learn the boundary: AI drafts and reviews, *you* say the words live. The reps are the point — using AI to skip practice is using AI to stay bad.

**You're ready when:** Every discovery call has an AI-generated research brief beforehand and a MEDDPICC scoring pass afterward. You've done at least one AI role-play before a difficult conversation, and it changed what you said in the real one.

### Practice This

Build three reusable prompts: (1) pre-call research brief from a company URL, (2) discovery role-play partner with an objection style you're weak against, (3) MEDDPICC scorecard generator from raw call notes. Use all three on your next real opportunity, then compare the AI's scorecard against your own honest one.
