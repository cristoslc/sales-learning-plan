1. Position the Offer is the gateway, and it feeds 2. Outbound and 3. Discovery: every message and question inherits its language from positioning. 3 and 4 are the discovery pair — question discipline feeds 4. Quantify the Pain, and both discovery outputs (5. Champion & Buyers included) feed 6. Qualify (MEDDPICC), the hinge that converts diagnosis into go/no-go evidence. Qualification feeds 7. Proposal & Paper Process and 9. Pipeline & Forecast; proposals feed 8. Negotiation. 10. Referrals & Second Deals compounds everything and loops back into positioning as proof. 11. AI Sales Partner is a force multiplier you use alongside everything.
